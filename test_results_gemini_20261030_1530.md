## Q7: What's the difference between PVC and XLPE cable?

**Expected:** PVC 70 degC, XLPE 90 degC, XLPE carries higher current for same size, per Cutix's catalogue.

**Answer:**

The main difference is the insulation material and temperature rating:

*   **PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride):** Rated for a maximum conductor temperature of 70°C.
*   **XLPE (Cross-linked Polyethylene):** A thermosetting material rated for 90°C.

Because XLPE withstands higher temperatures, it can carry more current than a PVC cable of the same size. Both are used for low-voltage power distribution.

Final selection should be confirmed by a qualified electrician or engineer.
