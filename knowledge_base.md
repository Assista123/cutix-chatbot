## 1. Company overview
- Cutix PLC manufactures electrical wires and cables: house wiring, power cables, aluminium conductors, bare copper conductors, automotive and appliance cables.
- Conceived in 1981 by Engr. (Dr.) Ajulu Uzodike, who left Raychem Corporation to set up an indigenous cable and switchgear company. Adtec Ltd (a venture capital and management firm, incorporated 1978) started the projects as divisions in 1982. Cutix was incorporated on November 4, 1982 as a private limited company.
- Mid-1983: 18 founding shareholders took over Adtec's cable division as Cutix Ltd. In 1987 (public issue concluded Aug 12, 1987), Cutix became the first private manufacturing company east of the Niger quoted on the Nigerian Stock Exchange, and became Cutix Plc.
- Head office and factory: No 17 Osita Onyejianya Street, Umuanuka Otolo, P.M.B. 5040, Nnewi, Anambra State. RC-50599.
- Staff installed and commissioned all machinery; Cutix describes this as a deliberate 100% Nigerian policy.
- Machinery timeline (brochure): first extrusion line 1984; second line 1985; first cable maker to acquire a plastic compounding line (1992); machines such as LBTS, F13, F-Shaw, armouring and cabling lines added 2000-2007; separate factory for armoured/non-armoured and XLPE power cables commissioned 2014; double twist buncher 2015; tubular strander and drum-to-drum rewinder commissioned April 2019; new 80mm extruder, extra rewinder and braiding machine (for solar cables) in 2022.
- Financial figures in the brochure are as of April 2022 and are dated. Do not quote them as current.
- Mission: to power and illuminate the world. Vision: to become one of the top three providers of electrical energy products and services in the world.
- Core values: Boldness, Openness, Excellence, Integrity, Proactiveness, Innovation.
- HSE policy: committed to healthy and safe operating conditions for employees, visitors and the host community, and to high environmental standards.
- Quality policy: exclusive-quality wires and cables and services meeting customer and regulatory requirements, with continual improvement of the quality management system, delivered on time and at competitive prices.
- Tagline: "Cutix Cables...Exclusive Quality".
- Leadership (verified from the company's NGX filing and press, March 2026): Mrs. Uchechukwu (Uche) Igbokwe is Acting CEO and Mrs. Uche Okonkwo is Acting CFO, both since March 5, 2026, pending permanent appointments. They replaced Mrs. Ijeoma Oduonye (CEO) and Mr. Chidi Onwudiwe (CFO), who left on that date. Igbokwe joined Cutix in 1992 and was previously Assistant General Manager and Head of Strategy and Planning in the CEO's office. Chairman: Barr. (Mrs.) Ifeoma Regina Nwahiri.

### Section Three: General technical information (selected)
- Catalogue covers low voltage 0.6/1 kV copper and aluminium power cables.
- Standards cited: NIS/IEC 60502-1 (PVC/XLPE insulated), BS 5467 (XLPE armoured), BS 7889 (XLPE non-armoured), BS 6346 (PVC insulated), NIS/IEC 60228 (conductors), NIS/IEC 60227 (PVC insulated).
- Conductors may be copper or aluminium; solid up to 4 mm2, round stranded from 4 up to 150 mm2, sector-shaped stranded from 50 up to 150 mm2 (some XLPE tables state differing ranges).
- XLPE is thermosetting and withstands up to 90 degC, so it carries higher current than PVC (70 degC) for the same conductor size.
- Standard core colours (NIS IEC 60502-1): 1-core red or black; 2-core red & black; 3-core red, yellow & blue; 4-core red, yellow, blue & black; 5-core adds green/yellow. BS 5467 colours (brown/blue/black/grey) available on request.
- Standard sheath colour is black; red and light blue available on request. Insulation includes UV-resistant masterbatch.
- Special options: anti-termite/anti-vermin additive; flame retardant to IEC 60332-1; a compound with oxygen index above 30% for IEC 60332-3 requirements; HDPE jacket for abrasion, water ingress and stress-crack resistance.
- Installation advice: PVC and XLPE LV cables are suitable indoors and outdoors; armoured cables are not recommended for tray applications (heavy); unarmoured cables are not recommended for direct burial unless designed and tested for it; HDPE jackets suit wet locations.
- Load current formulas given for single-phase, DC and three-phase AC; cross-section must carry at least the load current. Allowable voltage drop (IEC 60364-5-52): 3% lighting / 5% other when supplied from public power; 6% / 8% when from private supply. A voltage-drop table (mV/A/m per size, copper and aluminium) is in the brochure.
- Minimum bending radius table: unarmoured up to 25 mm OD is 8x overall diameter; over 25 mm OD is 10x; single-conductor with metallic shielding is 12x; multi-conductor armoured is 16x. (The same page text also says "6D"; see Conflicts.)
- Production: copper/aluminium rods are drawn, stranded, insulated/sheathed, cored, bedded, armoured and sheathed, with a QA test at every stage and final testing before dispatch.
- Quality lab: in-house quality assurance laboratory.

## 14. Bot behavior rules (draft v3; the system prompt file supersedes this)
1. Answer only from this knowledge base. If unsure, say so and give the nearest branch or main line.
2. No prices, discounts or stock levels.
3. 6. Lead capture: need, quantity, location, then route to the matching branch.
7. Reply in the user's language (English, Pidgin). Short answers, mobile first.
8. Orders and quotes: collect need, quantity and location, then point to the Orders page WhatsApp and the quote form.
